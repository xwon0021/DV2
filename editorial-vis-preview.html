What Australians say about buying local

Four separate findings from Roy Morgan’s March 2025 study.

84%

More likely to buy when they know a product is Australian Made.

Conditional stated likelihood — not measured purchases.

20 people = 100% · each person represents 5 percentage points of respondents. Partial fill shows the exact remainder. Symbols represent percentage points, not individual people.

Hover, focus or tap any row for the full wording, period and source.

84% report being more likely to buy a product when they know it is Australian Made. This is conditional reported likelihood, not observed purchasing.

“Want to buy more” includes reducing reliance on imports. Intention refers to the next 12 months; looking to buy more refers to the last 12 months. The 84% finding is conditional on knowing a product is Australian Made. Hover for source wording. These findings overlap and are not additive, funnel stages or observed purchases. The supplied PDF gives no sample size, sampling method or confidence intervals.
