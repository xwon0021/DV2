Manufacturing businesses per 10,000 residents

No valid rate

Read with care. In 2025, Canning Vale Commercial had 156 manufacturing businesses and an ERP of 5 residents. That equals 312,000 businesses per 10,000 residents. The tiny denominator makes this rate unstable; it does not mean there are 312,000 factories.

Manufacturing’s domestic economic connections

Supplying industry → using industry, 2023–24. Ribbon width represents domestic intermediate-use value.

Arrow tips point to the using division. Manufacturing → Manufacturing is self-use, not a reciprocal pair.

Hover or tap a division/ribbon, or use the controls. On narrow screens, scroll inside the chord to retain readable division labels.

All 37 directed relationships across 19 ANZSIC divisions are shown: A$444.361bn, 100% of the scoped value. Rows supply; columns use. Official division aggregation retains reciprocal relationships and manufacturing self-use. Arc spans allocate ribbon endpoints; they do not represent industry output. Non-manufacturing ↔ non-manufacturing relationships and final demand are outside this question. This is domestic industry accounting at basic prices, not company supply chains or physical goods movement.

Source: ABS Australian National Accounts: Input-Output Tables, 2023–24, Table 5, domestic intermediate use; official IOIG–ANZSIC division concordance. Tables and downloads · Methodology and concordance.

Visualisation 13

How industry value added changed

Each line connects the same subdivision’s IVA in 2023–24 and 2024–25. Both columns use a common vertical scale.

Select a subdivision or hover a line, endpoint or label to follow its change. On narrow screens, scroll inside the chart. Short leader lines separate labels from their true value positions.

Largest absolute movers: food IVA rose $2,348m to $31,279m; textiles/clothing fell $336m to $2,216m in 2024–25.

All 15 subdivisions have valid values and non-zero baselines. Subdivision totals are $135,725m and $141,974m, each $1m above the published manufacturing total because of rounding. IVA is economic contribution, not profit, sales or productivity. Dollar changes are unadjusted and may contain price effects. Amber identifies the selected subdivision, or the largest absolute increase and decline when no selection is active; slate retains comparison context.

Source: ABS Australian Industry, 2024–25, Table 1, subdivision IVA in 2023–24 and 2024–25, A$ million. Dataset and downloads

Compare versionsPreview ISOTYPE

Interest in Australian-made products

Four separate findings from Roy Morgan’s March 2025 study.

“Want to buy more” includes reducing reliance on imports. Intention refers to the next 12 months; looking to buy more refers to the last 12 months. The 84% finding is conditional on knowing a product is Australian Made. Hover for source wording. These findings overlap and are not additive, funnel stages or observed purchases. The supplied PDF gives no sample size, sampling method or confidence intervals.

Source: Roy Morgan Research, Roy Morgan Research 2025, study conducted March 2025, Australian Made Campaign Ltd infographic. Research infographic

Manufacturing location, employment, value added and exports describe different parts of the sector. Stated consumer interest adds context, but does not establish purchases or Australian Made certification.
